# Your profile photo goes here

Drop your photo into this folder (`public/`) and name it **`profile.jpg`**:

```
public/profile.jpg
```

The site loads it at `/profile.jpg`. Until you add it, the avatar gracefully falls back to a styled "MA" monogram, so nothing looks broken.

Recommended: a square or near-square headshot, at least 800×800px, JPG/WebP.
